# Luna chibi comparison

The canonical chibi design is the user-supplied front diagonal sprite at `art/references/luna_chibi_master.png`. It defines the figure proportions, hat and pompom, peach-pink band, face and glasses, hair, hoodie, boots, and crescent staff. The checkerboard is transparent in this copy and visible RGB is preserved. The cleaned reference was rendered in Godot and is the idle source for the updated front-diagonal walk.


Run `scenes/2d/luna_comparison.tscn` in Godot with F6. Use Tab or the upper-left button to swap between original and chibi, WASD or arrows to walk, and the wheel to compare at different sizes. Both variants share the camera, facing, walking speed and ground position. The comparison scene also supports running and casting. See `art/specs/luna_chibi_actions.md` for the chibi action bank and controls.

Chibi has five authored views and mirrors the left views. Each has two idle cels and two alternating contact cels. The planted idle pose doubles as the passing pose. The chibi has a nominal visible height of 224 world pixels at default size. The neutral meadow is a single 1472 × 1024 image cropped from the generated field without resampling.

Built-in ImageGen produced the sprite sheets and meadow. Raw sheets are under `art/generated/luna/chibi`. Runtime cels and pivots are under `assets/2d/characters/luna/chibi`. The grass texture is `assets/2d/eternia/pixel/comparison_meadow.png`. Sprite preparation cropped and cleaned alpha without resampling or redrawing.
