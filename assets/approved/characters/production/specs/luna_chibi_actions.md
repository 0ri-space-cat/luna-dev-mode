# Chibi Luna actions

Open `scenes/2d/luna_comparison.tscn` in Godot. Tab or the button swaps between the original Luna and chibi. WASD / arrows walk, Shift runs, F / left click casts. Q stows or retrieves the chibi wand for this comparison. The chibi wand state persists across Tab. The running art uses free hands. Chibi casts retain root movement, and the release chooses an authored walking contact while moving.

Each of the five authored views contains unarmed idle and walk, four running poses, a standing wind-up and release, and two moving releases. Left views mirror right views. The rear diagonal uses an authored passing pose and dedicated contacts with the leading shoe up-right. The original compact hat is copied above the waist across the three walking poses. Movement and registration are checked in Godot by `tests/test_luna_comparison.gd`.

Built-in ImageGen produced the source art. Raw files are in `art/generated/luna/chibi_actions`; game-ready files are in `assets/2d/characters/luna/chibi_actions`. `art/tools/prepare_luna_chibi_actions.py` uses Pillow, NumPy, and OpenCV for alpha, extraction, registration, a small pixel grid, and joining authored poses at the waist. The original project is on the same branch and this comparison scene shares its walking and sprint speeds.
